# Game-feel release — 24 October 2026

Separate Blackwater test build, version 0.10.0. Rules, card costs, card balance, existing base pictures and the approved arena image are preserved. The pending background video has not been substituted or generated.

Added drifting mist, a quiet water shimmer and lamp glows as decorative overlays. The water bowl now follows actual reserves; water motes show generation, spending, storage and silo refunds.

Attacks have anticipation, a short impact hold, a dash trail, impact rings and synchronized damage cues. Deaths gain a shock ring, smoke and a soft impact sound. Lane changes animate between slots without changing readiness or delaying gameplay. Newly deployed people have landing rings; Rush has a separate readiness cue. Multi-card draws animate correctly when returning to a player in local play.

Healing travels from Medic or the active healing base to the recipient. Barriers form and break visibly. Radio Operator shows its publicly revealed card and bottom/keep result. Confirmed event resolutions pulse their track and affected row or column; targeted tactics send a brief projectile. A base crossing five health shows the damaged cue, while destruction remains separate.

EFFECTS opens per-device sound, atmosphere and reduced-motion controls. Device reduced-motion settings are honored. Sound is softly synthesized in the browser after a user gesture; no external sound downloads are required. Ambient animation pauses in a hidden tab. Scheduled effects are cancelled on scene/match changes, and visual copies never intercept clicks.

Validation includes the full rule suite, read-only feedback planning, identical public-effect planning for either player, duplicate-state suppression, Medic healing, shields, Radio scanning, event resolution, three-card draws, base damage/destruction/victory, saved settings and a 1280 by 720 layout check. Visual QA scenarios run locally only and are excluded from the deployment.
